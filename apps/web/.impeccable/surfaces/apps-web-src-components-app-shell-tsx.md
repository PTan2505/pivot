# App shell: library, knowledge, sources, skills

Scope: the whole web app shell (top bar, section nav, library views, detail pages, overlays) plus the Chrome extension popup. Visitor mode: Operate. The owner saves inspiration and pulls it back while designing or briefing Claude Code. The task is to find, filter and hand off. The content is saved screenshots and notes, which must keep their true colour. Constraint: dark only, text at least 4.5:1.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The owner's light source is the work itself. The chrome is a dark kiln that the saved work glows out of: deep warm black, with orange light pooled behind frosted glass chrome. This refuses the category default of a neutral grey gallery with one blue accent.
OWN-WORLD: The ground is warm black #0c0806 with oxblood-brown surface steps (#18110d, #221812). Ink is warm white #fbf3ea. One ember orange #ff6a13 marks the primary action, with dark #0c0806 text. Orange-amber #ff8c42 is used for links, focus and selection. Frosted warm glass, with a 1px light edge and 24px blur, covers the chrome and overlays. Cards stay solid. Controls are pills.
STORY: The owner sees the section nav first, then the tag pills, then their screenshots. They save with one primary action and open menus that float as glass over the work.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A sticky glass top bar holds the logo, the section nav and the action pills, with the search field below it on phones. Ember light pools inside the glass behind the solid search field, and a glowing seam runs along the bar's lower edge. Below it are the display title and count, the tag pills, the Type/Sort row, then the grid of solid 16:10 cards. The orange Add button is at the top-bar's right end.
FORM: The signature move is ember light caught in the glass top bar (amber core, orange body, deep-red falloff) behind the solid search field, plus a glowing seam along the bar's lower edge, like light through the gaps between tiles. It never sits under page text, because any visible glow drops small grey text below 4.5:1. The list position is 1; the owner pinned the direction from a reference. seed: none (pinned by owner)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; finish review and documentation before the final report.
